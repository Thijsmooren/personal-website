The belief

Quantum is not a quarterly promise. It is a strategic head start.

There is no real quantum advantage yet. That is exactly why the preparation matters now.

The expensive work comes first.

Formulating a business problem into a quantum-ready workflow takes years. Hardware maturity and organisational readiness arrive on different schedules.

The five-year-old test

Make the wrong answers cancel out.

A quantum computer holds possibilities in a blurry way, then arranges the question so the useful answer is the one left shouting.
